**DPDP compliance, built for India.** Built for how India actually does business —
your customer's language, your billing software, offline counters — not a GDPR tool
with a DPDP module bolted on.

#### What the EasyDP platform does

- **Consent** — capture valid consent and keep a log of who agreed to what, and when.
- **Your customer's language** — send the notices the law requires in every major Indian language.
- **Customer requests** — handle requests to see, correct or delete data, start to finish.
- **Data breaches** — a step-by-step 72-hour breach workflow.
- **Inspection-ready** — an audit trail of consents, notices, requests and breaches.
